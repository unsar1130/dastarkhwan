Our Story

A family table, open to the whole city

A dastarkhwan is the cloth spread for a shared meal, where everyone is welcome. Karachi Dastarkhwan began in 1987 as a small nihari stall on Burns Road and grew into a family restaurant that still cooks every dish from scratch.

Our nihari simmers overnight, our biryani is sealed and cooked on dum, and our kebabs are grilled over real charcoal. Spices are ground in-house every morning, and all our meat is halal.
